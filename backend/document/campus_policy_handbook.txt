1. MINIMUM ATTENDANCE & CONDONATION POLICY
- All enrolled students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in lecture, tutorial, and laboratory sessions across each course.
- Condonation of shortage of attendance between 65% and 74% may be granted strictly on valid medical grounds, approved university sports participation, or sanctioned academic deputations.
- Medical certificates must be countersigned by the Chief Medical Officer at the Campus Health Centre within 7 days of returning to campus.
- Students with attendance below 65% are strictly debarred from sitting for the semester end examinations in that course.

2. EXAMINATION & GRADING REGULATIONS
- Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) accounts for 40% of the total course marks.
- End-Semester Examinations account for 60% of total course marks.
- Passing Standard: A student must score at least 40% in internal assessments and 40% in end-semester examinations independently to pass.
- Letter Grades & Point Scale:
  * O (Outstanding): 90-100% -> Grade Point 10
  * A+ (Excellent): 80-89% -> Grade Point 9
  * A (Very Good): 70-79% -> Grade Point 8
  * B+ (Good): 60-69% -> Grade Point 7
  * B (Above Average): 55-59% -> Grade Point 6
  * C (Average): 50-54% -> Grade Point 5
  * P (Pass): 40-49% -> Grade Point 4
  * F (Fail): Below 40% -> Grade Point 0 (Requires Supplementary/Backlog Exam)

3. HOSTEL RESIDENTIAL NORMS & CURFEW TIMINGS
- First-year undergraduate hostel curfew is strictly 9:30 PM.
- Senior students and postgraduates have hostel in-timings until 10:30 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends.
- Outstation night leaves require an online pass generated on the Student Portal, verified by parents via automated SMS authorization 24 hours prior.
- Quiet study hours are observed from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM daily.

4. SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
- Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship: 100% tuition waiver for students with family income under $4,000 / Rs 3.5 Lakhs and CGPA >= 8.5.
- Dean's Honour List Scholarship: Top 2% academic rank holders in each branch receive a cash award and certificate of distinction.
- Application portal opens on September 1st and closes October 15th annually.

5. CAMPUS WI-FI & IT HELPDESK
- High-speed 5GHz Eduroam and CampusSecure Wi-Fi is available across all academic blocks, hostels, and libraries.
- Maximum 2 registered devices per student.
- IT Helpdesk is located at Computer Centre, Room 105. Helpline: support@university.edu or extension 4410.

6. ZERO-TOLERANCE ANTI-RAGGING & STUDENT SAFETY
- Ragging in any form is a punishable non-bailable offense.
- 24/7 Anti-Ragging Emergency Helpline: 1800-111-SAFE (7233).
- Direct liaison with local police station and campus security dispatch within 3 minutes of alert.
